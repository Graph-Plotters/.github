# Graph Plotters

**See the math move.** A free, client-side online graphing calculator and interactive function toolkit built for students, teachers, and learners at [graphplotters.com](https://graphplotters.com)—with zero accounts or downloads required.

### 📈 Interactive Graphing & Math Tools
* **[Online Graphing Calculator](https://graphplotters.com)** — Plot multiple equations simultaneously, inspect live `(x, y)` coordinates on hover, zoom/pan, export PNG/SVG graphs, and share setups via URL.
* **[Polynomial & Algebraic Graphs](https://graphplotters.com)** — Explore **Linear**, **Quadratic**, **Cubic**, **Absolute Value**, **Reciprocal**, **Exponential Growth**, and **Logarithmic** graphs.
* **[Trigonometric Graphs](https://graphplotters.com)** — Visualize **Sine**, **Cosine**, **Tangent**, **Secant**, and **Cosecant** waves, asymptotes, and repeating cycles.
* **[Function Properties & Transformations](https://graphplotters.com)** — Analyze **Transformations** (shifts, stretches, reflections) and calculate **Slope**, **X-Intercepts**, **Vertex**, and **Phase Shift**.

### ✨ Key Highlights
* **100% Client-Side & Private:** Runs directly in your browser on desktop, tablet, or mobile with light/dark themes and an on-screen math keyboard.
* **Built for Understanding:** Keeps key properties, intercepts, and turning points visible alongside the plotted curves.
